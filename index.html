SMS Policy

Governing SMS communications sent through services managed by Travis Wessman on behalf of clients at traviswessman.dev.

Service Description

Messages are sent by or on behalf of businesses that have engaged traviswessman.dev for communication services. Uses include missed call notifications, appointment confirmations, and service-related updates. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Consent & Opt-In

By providing your phone number to a business using our SMS services, you consent to receive text messages from that business. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies by business.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one final confirmation message, then no further messages unless you opt back in by replying START.

Help & Support

Reply HELP to any message, or contact:

travis@traviswessman.dev

Privacy

Phone numbers are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. They are used solely for the described communication services.

Message Frequency

For missed call auto-responses: one (1) message per missed call, sent once per caller per session. Frequency varies by business.

Carrier Disclaimer

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
