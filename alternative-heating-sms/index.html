SMS Policy & Privacy — Alternative Heating Solutions

This SMS program is operated by Wessman Systems on behalf of Alternative Heating Solutions. It is a single-purpose program used only to return missed calls; it is not a shared or multi-business service.

How you opt in

You opt in by calling Alternative Heating Solutions at 508-455-0053 — the number published on the company website, Google Business listing, and vehicle/business signage. If we cannot answer, you receive one automated text reply about that call, inviting you to reply with your name, address, and service needed. No purchase required; consent is not a condition of purchase.

Sample message

Alternative Heating Solutions: Thanks for calling Alternative Heating Solutions. We missed you — reply with your name, address, and what you need, and we'll call you back. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message frequency

One automated reply per missed call, plus replies to messages you send us. Frequency may vary.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or call 508-455-0053.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You'll receive one confirmation, then no further messages unless you reply START.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for marketing. Numbers are used solely to return your call and coordinate the requested service. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
